Monograph

People, Power and Planning:
Participatory Approaches in Mining Regions

Edited by: Tamara Maričić, Marijana Pantić, Ana Perić

The book seeks to contribute to the growing international discussion on the necessity of meaningful citizen and community involvement in the planning, governance and implementation of mining and energy transition processes.
